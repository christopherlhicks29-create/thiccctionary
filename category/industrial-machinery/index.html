Industrial Machinery

26 catalogued entries

Industrial equipment is the section where the editorial standard is tested hardest, because industry produces a great many objects that are merely heavy. Heavy is not thiccc. A crane is enormous and almost entirely absent. What qualifies here is the machine whose working mass sits in one continuous body: the drum, the flywheel, the cutting head. Tonnage is a specification. Girth is a silhouette.
